About ClearCalc

ClearCalc is a free collection of financial calculators built to help people make everyday money decisions with confidence, whether that's taking out a loan, saving for a goal, or setting a freelance rate.

Each calculator uses standard, published formulas and explains how it works, so you can check the maths yourself. All calculations happen in your browser; we never receive the numbers you type.

Our approach

  • Clear inputs with sensible defaults
  • Results you can understand at a glance, with the detail available when you want it
  • Support for many currencies, for users around the world
  • No account, no sign-up, no paywall

The site is supported by advertising, which keeps every tool free to use.

Important note

Our calculators give estimates for information and education only. They are not financial, tax or legal advice. For decisions that matter, check the figures with your lender, bank or a qualified adviser.
