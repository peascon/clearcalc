How to add VAT

Multiply the net price by 1 plus the VAT rate:

Gross = Net × (1 + rate ÷ 100)

Example: a product costs 10,000 before VAT, and the rate is 18%. The VAT is 10,000 × 0.18 = 1,800, so the customer pays 11,800.

How to remove VAT

A very common mistake is to take 18% off the total. That gives the wrong answer, because the VAT was calculated on the net price, not the total. Divide instead:

Net = Gross ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100)

Example: a receipt shows 11,800 including 18% VAT. The net price is 11,800 ÷ 1.18 = 10,000, and the VAT inside it is 1,800. Taking 18% off 11,800 would wrongly give 9,676.

A shortcut for the VAT amount inside a total is Gross × rate ÷ (100 + rate). At 18%, that's the total × 18/118.

Frequently asked questions

Is VAT the same as GST or sales tax?
GST (goods and services tax) works the same way as VAT and uses the same maths, so this calculator works for GST too. US sales tax is different: it's set by states and cities and only charged at the final sale, but adding it to a price uses the same formula.
When does a business have to charge VAT?
Usually once its yearly sales pass a registration threshold set by the tax authority. The threshold differs by country, so check your tax authority's website.
What is the VAT rate in Rwanda?
The standard VAT rate in Rwanda is 18%. Some goods and services, such as exports, are zero-rated or exempt.
Why does my total not match exactly?
Shops and invoicing systems sometimes round VAT per item instead of on the total, which can cause differences of a few cents or francs.
